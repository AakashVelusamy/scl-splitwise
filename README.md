## Overview
The **Splitwise Lite** application is designed to simplify how groups manage shared expenses. Whether you're dining out with friends, sharing bills in a flat, or splitting travel costs, this app helps you track who owes what, making financial interactions smooth and stress-free.

## Key Features
- **Add Expenses**  
  Users can easily record expenses by specifying the payer and participants, allowing the app to automatically calculate everyone's share. This feature simplifies tracking contributions.

- **View Balances**  
  Provides a clear overview of current balances, indicating who owes money and who is owed. This transparency fosters clarity among group members and helps maintain healthy financial relationships.

- **Simplify Debts**  
  The app offers a feature to simplify debts, allowing users to see straightforward transactions required to settle balances, thereby reducing complexity and confusion.

- **Settle Up**  
  When members decide to clear their debts, they can easily record transactions between debtors and creditors. This function ensures that balances are updated in real time, reflecting the current financial status of the group.

- **Visualize Expenses**  
  Includes graphical representations of debts, helping users visualize their financial interactions clearly and engagingly.

## Summary
Splitwise Lite is designed to make managing shared expenses effortless, enabling you and your friends or family to enjoy each other's company without the stress of financial disagreements. By simplifying the process of tracking who owes what, the app allows everyone to contribute fairly to shared bills, ultimately keeping relationships intact while handling financial matters with ease.
